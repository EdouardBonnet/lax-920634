We construct [arbitrarily large subcubic expanders](Lax920634.ExpanderExistence.html)
and deduce [subcubic graphs of linear treewidth](Lax920634.TreewidthExistence.html).
The deterministic construction uses a fixed seed, zig-zag products, and path
replacement. A [uniform word-RAM algorithm](Lax920634.LinearConstruction.html)
outputs an $N$-vertex member in $O(N)$ time using $O(\log(N+1))$-bit words.
The fixed constants are enormous.
