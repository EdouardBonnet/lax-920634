We specify a deterministic family of finite simple subcubic graphs on
$8(m+1)^2$ vertices, obtained by replacing each vertex of the
Margulis–Gabber–Galil multigraph with an eight-cycle. Explicit modular formulas
compute each vertex's three neighbors. The family has edge expansion at
least $1/128$ and satisfies
$|V|\le1536(\operatorname{tw}(G)+1)$, giving arbitrarily large subcubic graphs
of treewidth at least $|V|/3072$.

The statements separate the modular base expansion, cycle replacement, and
expansion-to-treewidth arguments. Their Lean proof status is recorded in the
submission README.
